Create a CSV with the following columns: languageid, letter, phonetic_value.

The languageid will be one of: greek, hebrew, arabic, sanskrit.

Each row contains one letter form. For scripts with positional variants (Arabic),
use the actual Unicode positional glyphs. For scripts with final-only variants
(Greek sigma, Hebrew sofit letters), include both standard and final forms.

Dash notation indicates where the rest of the word would appear visually:
- x    = isolated (standalone letter)
- -x   = initial (rest of word is to the right of the dash)
- -x-  = medial (letters on both sides)
- x-   = final (rest of word is to the left of the dash)

For RTL scripts (Hebrew, Arabic), the rest of the word is visually to the RIGHT
of a final letter and to the LEFT of an initial letter.
