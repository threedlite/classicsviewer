# Privacy Policy for Classics Viewer

**Last Updated: September 25, 2025**

## Introduction

Classics Viewer ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our iOS application.

Classics Viewer is a reader application for classical Greek and Latin texts from the Perseus Digital Library, designed to operate offline without requiring internet access.

## Information We Collect

### No Personal Information Collected

Classics Viewer does **NOT** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app operates entirely offline and includes:

- No user accounts or registration
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising identifiers
- No location services
- No camera or photo library access
- No microphone access
- No contact information access
- No network communication
- No third-party services or SDKs that collect data

### Local Data Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

1. **Database Files**: Classical texts database (Perseus Digital Library texts)
2. **User Preferences**: Reading settings such as:
   - Selected language (Greek/Latin)
   - Font size preferences
   - Color theme settings
   - Display preferences
3. **Reading Progress**:
   - Bookmarks you create
   - Last read positions in texts
   - User-created notes (if applicable)
4. **Custom Dictionary Entries**: Any personal vocabulary notes you add

All this data remains on your device and is never transmitted to any external servers.

## How We Use Information

Since we don't collect any personal information, we don't use any information about you. The app functions entirely offline using:

- Pre-packaged classical texts from the Perseus Digital Library (public domain)
- Local SQLite database for text storage and retrieval
- Device storage for user preferences and reading progress

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **NOT** share any data with third parties because:

- No data is collected from users
- No network connections are made
- No external services are integrated
- The app operates 100% offline

## Data Security

Your data security is ensured through:

- **Complete Offline Operation**: No internet connection means no data transmission risks
- **Local Storage Only**: All data stays on your device
- **iOS Sandboxing**: Data is protected by iOS's built-in app sandboxing
- **No Cloud Sync**: Local only

## File Sharing Feature

The app enables iOS File Sharing to allow you to:
- Import custom database files (.db or .zip format)
- Export your bookmarks or notes (if applicable)
- Backup your reading progress

These files are only accessible through iTunes/Finder file sharing when your device is connected to your computer. No automatic uploads or network transfers occur.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- All data is stored locally on your device
- Uninstalling the app removes all associated data
- You can clear app data at any time through iOS Settings
- No data persists after app removal
