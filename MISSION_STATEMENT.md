The purpose of the Classics Viewer app is to make ancient texts in the original languages easily accessible to beginning and intermediate students of those languages, conviently on mobile devices.
To encourage collaboration, only sources with open source licenses such as CC-BY-SA, MIT, and public domain are used. Restrictive licenses such as CC-BY-SA-NC or GPL cannot be used.
The libraries of Ashurbanipal and Ptolemy I Soter gathered and translated texts, with an emphasis on wide scope.  This is an attempt to emulate that model, albeit on a less durable medium than clay or papyrus.
