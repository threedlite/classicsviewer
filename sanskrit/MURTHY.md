  Summary: 2 out of 5 Murty texts are in our Sanskrit collection:
  - Yājñavalkyasmṛti (Treatise on Dharma)
  - Kirātārjunīya (Arjuna and the Hunter)

  The missing ones are:
  - Raghuvaṃśa - Kalidasa's epic poem about the Raghu dynasty (though we have 3 other Kalidasa works)
  - Śiśupālavadha - Magha's mahākāvya about Krishna killing Shishupala
  - Nītisāra - Kamandaki's treatise on statecraft and politics
