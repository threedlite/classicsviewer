# Iliad Text Notes

## Line Count

- **Traditional count**: 15,693 lines
- **Perseus critical text**: 15,687 lines
- **Difference**: 6 lines (athetized)

## Athetized Lines

The Perseus Digital Library uses a critical text that omits lines considered spurious interpolations by modern scholarship. The traditional line numbering is preserved, creating gaps.

### Book 9, Lines 458-461 (4 lines)

**Context**: Phoenix's story about his father's curse

```
457: Ζεύς τε καταχθόνιος καὶ ἐπαινὴ Περσεφόνεια.
     (Zeus of the underworld and dread Persephone)
     [OMITTED: 458-461]
462: ἔνθʼ ἐμοὶ οὐκέτι πάμπαν ἐρητύετʼ ἐν φρεσὶ θυμὸς
     (Then my spirit could no longer be restrained)
```

These lines described Phoenix's father praying to the Furies. Considered a later expansion of the curse narrative.

### Book 11, Line 543 (1 line)

**Context**: Hector avoiding Ajax, then Zeus intervening

```
542: Αἴαντος δʼ ἀλέεινε μάχην Τελαμωνιάδαο.
     (He avoided battle with Telamonian Ajax)
     [OMITTED: 543]
544: Ζεὺς δὲ πατὴρ Αἴανθʼ ὑψίζυγος ἐν φόβον ὦρσε·
     (Father Zeus on high drove Ajax to flight)
```

The missing line likely repeated or elaborated on the avoidance theme. Considered redundant.

### Book 14, Line 269 (1 line)

**Context**: Hera promising Sleep one of the Graces as wife

```
268: δώσω ὀπυιέμεναι καὶ σὴν κεκλῆσθαι ἄκοιτιν.
     (I will give her to wed and to be called your wife)
     [OMITTED: 269]
270: ὣς φάτο, χήρατο δʼ Ὕπνος, ἀμειβόμενος δὲ προσηύδα·
     (So she spoke, and Sleep rejoiced and answered)
```

The missing line probably named the specific Grace (Pasithea is mentioned later at 276). Considered an interpolation.
